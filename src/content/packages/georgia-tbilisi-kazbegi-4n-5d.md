The one addition that changes a Georgia trip most is Kazbegi. It is a
long day — the Georgian Military Highway north, a reservoir, a
fortress, a pass, and then a 4x4 up to a church on a ridge — but it is
the day people come home talking about.

## Day by day

### Day 1 — Arrival

Arrival in Tbilisi, transfer, check-in. Evening free.

### Day 2 — Tbilisi city tour

Old Town, Narikala, the Bridge of Peace, and the funicular up to
Mtatsminda.

### Day 3 — Kazbegi, full day

Zhinvali reservoir, Ananuri Fortress, the Gudauri viewpoint,
Stepantsminda, and Gergeti Trinity Church by 4x4.

### Day 4 — Mtskheta

Day trip to Mtskheta, then a free afternoon in Tbilisi.

### Day 5 — Departure

Checkout, airport transfer, departure.

## Good to know

Kazbegi is a full day and a long one — the road climbs to the Jvari
Pass at 2,379 metres and the last stretch to the church is unpaved,
which is why it is a 4x4 rather than the coach. In winter the pass can
close at short notice; we will say so when you book rather than on the
morning.
