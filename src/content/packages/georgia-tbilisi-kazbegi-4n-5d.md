The one addition that changes a Georgia trip most is Kazbegi. It is a
long day — the Georgian Military Highway north, a reservoir, a
fortress, a pass, and then a 4x4 up to a church on a ridge — but it is
the day people come home talking about.

Five days is also the first length at which the trip stops feeling
rushed: a city day, a mountain day, and a half-day to Mtskheta with an
afternoon left over.
