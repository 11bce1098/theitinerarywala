Eight days is the first length at which Georgia's two halves both fit.
Tbilisi and the mountains are one country; Batumi, on the Black Sea
with palm trees and a skyline, is visibly another. Crossing between
them is most of what makes the longer trip worth it.

## Good to know

Day 5 is the long one either way. The drive from Tbilisi to Batumi is
roughly six hours and takes in Mtskheta on the way out; the internal
flight is under an hour but puts you in Batumi with the afternoon
still to use. Which one suits depends on the season and on how much
driving you have already done — say which you would prefer when you
enquire.

Ali & Nino is the moving sculpture on the seafront: two seven-metre
steel figures that pass through each other and apart again every
evening.
