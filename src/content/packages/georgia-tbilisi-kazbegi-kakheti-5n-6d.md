Six days is the length at which Georgia stops being a city break and
starts being the country. You get the mountain day and the wine day,
which pull in opposite directions — north into the Caucasus, east into
the vineyards — with Tbilisi in the middle to come back to.

## Day by day

### Day 1 — Arrival

Arrival in Tbilisi, transfer, check-in. Evening free.

### Day 2 — Tbilisi city tour

### Day 3 — Kazbegi, full day

Ananuri, Gudauri, and Gergeti Trinity Church.

### Day 4 — Kakheti wine, full day

Sighnaghi, the "City of Love", Bodbe Monastery, and a winery visit
with tasting.

### Day 5 — Mtskheta

Half-day tour of Mtskheta, then a free afternoon.

### Day 6 — Departure

Checkout, airport transfer, departure.

## Good to know

The two full days sit back to back here, which is deliberate — Kakheti
is an easy day after a hard one, mostly flat driving and sitting down
to taste. Sighnaghi is walled and small, and the view from it runs out
over the Alazani valley to the Greater Caucasus you were standing in
the day before.
