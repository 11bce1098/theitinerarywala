Four days is a long weekend, and Tbilisi is one of the few capitals
where that is genuinely enough. The old town is small enough to cross
on foot, the fortress above it is a cable-car ride rather than a climb,
and the two places most worth leaving the city for — Mtskheta and
Ananuri — sit on the same road north.

This is the shortest of the Georgia packages, and it stays in and
around Tbilisi on purpose. The mountains need a full day each way and
do not fit honestly into four. If Kazbegi is the reason you are
coming, start at [the 4 nights, 5 days
package](/packages/georgia-tbilisi-kazbegi-4n-5d/) instead.
