Four days is a long weekend, and Tbilisi is one of the few capitals
where that is genuinely enough. The old town is small enough to cross
on foot, the fortress above it is a cable-car ride rather than a climb,
and the two places most worth leaving the city for — Mtskheta and
Ananuri — sit on the same road north.

## Day by day

### Day 1 — Arrival

Arrival in Tbilisi, airport transfer, check-in. Evening free at
leisure: an Old Town walk, or Shardeni Street.

### Day 2 — Tbilisi city tour

Old Town, Narikala Fortress by cable car, Metekhi Church, the Bridge
of Peace, and the sulfur bath district.

### Day 3 — Mtskheta and Ananuri

Day trip to Mtskheta for Jvari Monastery and Svetitskhoveli Cathedral,
then Ananuri Fortress.

### Day 4 — Departure

Free morning for shopping, checkout, airport transfer, departure.

## Good to know

This is the shortest of the Georgia packages, and it stays in and
around Tbilisi on purpose — the mountains need a full day each way and
do not fit honestly into four. If Kazbegi is the reason you are coming,
start at [the 4 nights, 5 days
package](/packages/georgia-tbilisi-kazbegi-4n-5d/) instead.
