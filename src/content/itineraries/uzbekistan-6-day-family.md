Uzbekistan is the easiest Silk Road introduction: the three cities worth
seeing sit on one fast rail line, so the trip is three hotels and no
long drives.

## Day 1 — Tashkent: arrive and take the metro

Land, check in, and spend the afternoon on the metro — the older stations
are worth seeing in their own right, and it is the cheapest way to get a
feel for the city before heading east.
