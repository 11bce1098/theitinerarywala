Georgia is the easiest "real mountains" trip you can do from the UAE:
a 3-hour flight, no visa paperwork for UAE residents on Indian passports,
and prices that make a week cost less than a weekend staycation at home.
This plan keeps drives under two hours a day, which is the difference
between a holiday and a hostage situation when you travel with a
preschooler.

## Day 1 — Land in Tbilisi, Old Town on foot

Ease in. Check in near the Old Town so everything is walkable, ride the
cable car up to Narikala Fortress (kids love it, queues are short before
noon), and finish at a courtyard restaurant with khinkali and lemonade.

## Day 2 — Mtskheta half-day trip

The old capital is 30 minutes away — a perfect first excursion.
Jvari Monastery for the view, Svetitskhoveli Cathedral for the scale,
back in Tbilisi by nap time.

<a class="book-btn" href="#PASTE-VIATOR-TOUR-LINK">Book a Mtskheta half-day tour</a>

## Day 3 — Drive to Kazbegi

The Georgian Military Highway is the most scenic road in the country.
Stop at Ananuri Fortress and the Russia–Georgia Friendship Monument
viewpoint. Overnight in Stepantsminda with Mount Kazbek out the window.

<a class="book-btn" href="#PASTE-VIATOR-TOUR-LINK">Book a private driver for the highway</a>

## Day 4 — Gergeti Trinity Church

Take a 4x4 up to the church (skip the hike with small kids — the
drive takes 20 minutes and the view is the same). Slow afternoon:
playground in the village square, khachapuri, early night.

## Day 5 — Back to Tbilisi via Chronicle of Georgia

Return south with a stop at the Chronicle of Georgia — massive
pillars, almost no crowds, and the single most dramatic photo stop
of the trip.

## Day 6 — Kakheti wine country day trip

Sighnaghi is Georgia's prettiest town and completely stroller-friendly
along the walls. Adults get a tasting; kids get grape juice from the
same vineyards. It works.

<a class="book-btn" href="#PASTE-VIATOR-TOUR-LINK">Book a Kakheti family day tour</a>

## Day 7 — Souvenirs and fly home

Dry Bridge Market for souvenirs in the morning, then out. Evening
flights land in the UAE at a reasonable bedtime.

### What this actually costs

For two adults and one child over 7 nights: mid-range hotels
(~AED 350/night), one private driver day, two group tours, and food
comes to roughly AED 2,800 per person, flights excluded. Book the
Kazbegi hotel first — the good ones with views sell out weeks ahead
in summer.
