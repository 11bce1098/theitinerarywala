Japan rewards staying put. Two bases over a week means one train journey
rather than five, and the Shinkansen between them is short enough that
the travel day still has an afternoon in it.

## Day 1 — Tokyo: land, settle, and stay close to the station

Pick a hotel near a JR loop station so the rest of the week is simple.
Keep the first evening light — a neighbourhood walk and an early dinner
beats trying to see anything after a long flight.
