The trip you keep not taking is the one on your doorstep. Four days is
enough to see the UAE as a visitor sees it rather than as a commuter —
and with no flights, no visas and no airport, the whole thing starts on
a Thursday evening.

## Day 1 — Dubai: Creek, souks and the abra across

Start where the city did. The textile and gold souks either side of the
Creek, an abra across for a dirham, and dinner in Al Seef — all of it
walkable, and none of it the version of Dubai you drive past on the way
to work.
