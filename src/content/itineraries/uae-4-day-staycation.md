Four days is enough to see past the skyline. The emirates sit close
enough together that you can trade a beach morning for a mountain
afternoon without a long drive, which makes the UAE an unusually easy
short trip — and a good stopover if you are breaking a longer journey.

## Day 1 — Dubai: Creek, souks and the abra across

Start where the city did. The textile and gold souks either side of the
Creek, an abra across for a dirham, and dinner in Al Seef — all of it
walkable, and none of it the version of Dubai you drive past on the way
to work.
